Grow

This project is a website aimed at selling organic and healthy foods under the brand “Grow”. The design reflects values such as sustainability, freshness and healthy living, incorporating visual elements related to fruits, vegetables and natural colors that reinforce its ecological identity.

My participation in this project was not as the author of the original design, but as part of a practical exercise.
