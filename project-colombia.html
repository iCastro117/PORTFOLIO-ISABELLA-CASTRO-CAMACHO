Colombia Canta y Encanta

This project is a UX/UI redesign of the official website for “Colombia Canta y Encanta”, a folk festival celebrating Colombian culture through music and dance.

The goal was to modernize the visual identity and simplify the journey for participants, with clear sections to join the contest, browse past events, and share the experience on social media.
