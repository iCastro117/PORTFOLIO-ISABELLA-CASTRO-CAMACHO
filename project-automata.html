The project involves the design and construction of a functional automaton structure by manually assembling parts. This process includes making cuts, folding and joining parts following detailed instructions.

The objective is to create a mechanical model that simulates movement, combining basic mechanical principles with precision and creativity. This project has an educational focus, fostering skills such as patience, manual work and the interpretation of technical plans and instructions.
